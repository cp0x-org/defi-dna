Protocol overview

MetaDeFi DNA registry · edited Sep 19, 2026

Aave is a non-custodial liquidity protocol: users supply assets to earn interest and borrow against over-collateralized positions. Markets are deployed per chain and configured by the Aave DAO through Aave Governance V3. The DAO also issues the GHO stablecoin, minted against collateral supplied to Aave.
